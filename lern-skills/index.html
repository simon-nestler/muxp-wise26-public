Skills for Claude and other AI assistants

You can use these skills with Claude or another AI assistant. Download the folder you need and hand it to your assistant.

  • mxup-study-partner
    Practice partner for the module Mobile UX Prototyping (M.Sc. User Experience Design, TH Ingolstadt, winter term 2026/27). Always use when questions about this module's topics, exercise sheets U1–U6, the seminar paper (Part D), or the presentation (Parts A, B and the final presentation, Part C) come up.
  • mxup-topic9-offline
    Topic skill for "Offline states and sync conflicts" (Topic 9, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use in addition to the module's study partner when working on offline triage, sync conflicts, the offline part of Exercise U5, or an offline scope in the seminar paper. Topic 9 has no in-class session this term, so this skill supports the self-study from the script.
  • mxup-topic10-darkpatterns
    Topic skill for "Dark patterns: naming and judging" (Topic 10, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use in addition to the module's study partner when working on dark patterns, the triple filing, the dark-pattern part of Exercise U5, dark-pattern findings in the seminar paper, or the Brussels-hearing preparation (Topic 11).
  • mxup-checkpoint-prep
    Interim skill for the two presentation checkpoints, Part A and Part B (Mobile UX Prototyping): 15 minutes, about ten slides, in your team with the lecturer. Use when a student or team prepares the slides and the talk for Part A (within Exercise U2) or Part B (within Exercise U4) and wants them tested for defensibility before the upload deadline.
  • mxup-defense-rehearsal
    Interim skill for rehearsing the final presentation (Part C, Mobile UX Prototyping) on your own. Use from the moment the question pool opens until Part C, when the student wants a mock Q&A on their project along the defense anatomy and the open pool, or wants to prepare and debrief the self-organized mock round with the anchor sheet. There is no rehearsal session in the timetable; this skill supports rehearsing outside lectures and exercises.
  • mxup-evidence-check
    Interim skill for evidence discipline (Mobile UX Prototyping). Use when the student wants their own claims checked against the module's three-part criterion – on exercise sheets from Exercise U1 onward, on slides for Parts A, B and C, in the seminar paper (Part D), or anywhere a market claim appears.
  • mxup-expose-sparring
    Milestone skill for the exposé of the seminar paper (due end of week 5, after Topic 5, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use when the student wants to test their design question and fidelity decision – the fidelity block from Exercise U2 – for defensibility before submitting the exposé.
  • mxup-paper-check
    Interim skill for the seminar paper (Part D, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use when the student wants their draft checked criterion by criterion against the course's grading anchors S1–S12 from the self-assessment sheet – from the interim state (end of the Topic 10 week) until submission.
