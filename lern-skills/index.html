Skills for Claude and other AI assistants

You can use these skills with Claude or another AI assistant. Download the folder you need and hand it to your assistant.

  • mxup-study-partner
    Practice partner for the module Mobile UX Prototyping (MUXP, UXDM_MXUP; M.Sc. User Experience Design, TH Ingolstadt, winter term 2026/27). Always use when questions about this module's topics, exercise sheets U1–U5, the presentation (Part A, Part B and the final presentation, Part C) or the seminar paper come up. The module's topic and interim skills build on this one.
  • mxup-topic1-autopsy
    Topic skill for "Claims and their sources" (Topic 1, Mobile UX Prototyping) – the autopsy of an AI market analysis you generated yourself with the session's fixed prompt. Use in addition to the module's study partner when a student sorts their run (or their team's run from the Padlet, column T01) into the four quadrants correct / plausible-but-wrong / missing / hidden assumption, catches up on the autopsy after missing Topic 1, does the solo version for their own field, or rehearses the pool question on spotting wrong claims without research.
  • mxup-topic9-offline
    Topic skill for "Offline states and sync conflicts" (Topic 9, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use in addition to the module's study partner when working on offline triage, sync conflicts, the offline part of exercise sheet U5, or the offline check every concept gets in Part C and the seminar paper (S9). Topic 9 has no in-class session this term, so this skill supports the self-study from the script.
  • mxup-topic10-darkpatterns
    Topic skill for "Dark patterns: naming and judging" (Topic 10, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use in addition to the module's study partner when working on dark patterns, the triple filing, the borderline case from the bridge at the end of Topic 8, the dark-pattern part of exercise sheet U5, dark-pattern findings in Part C or the seminar paper (S10), or the Brussels hearing (Topic 11).
  • mxup-checkpoint-prep
    Interim skill for the two short graded presentations, Part A and Part B (Mobile UX Prototyping): 15 minutes in your team with the lecturer. Use when a team prepares slides and talk for Part A (results of exercise sheet U1; upload Friday 23 October, presented in Exercise U2) or Part B (results of sheets U2 and U3; upload Friday 20 November, presented in Exercise U4) and wants them tested before the upload. For the four elements of the fidelity block, use fidelity-sparring first; for Part C, use defense-rehearsal.
  • mxup-defense-rehearsal
    Interim skill for preparing the final presentation, Part C (Mobile UX Prototyping), on your own – 15 to 20 minutes per team in front of the exercise group plus a moderated discussion, all five exercise sheets U1–U5 relevant, upload Friday 18 December. Use when a team tests its core argument and talk, wants a mock Q&A along the defense anatomy and the open question pool, or prepares and debriefs the self-organized mock round with the anchor sheet.
  • mxup-evidence-check
    Interim skill for evidence discipline (Mobile UX Prototyping). Use when a student wants their own market claims checked against the module's three-part criterion (measurer, year, population) – the claim checks of exercise sheet U1 that Part A presents, slides for Parts A, B and C, the seminar paper (S1), or anywhere a market claim appears. For dissecting an AI-generated market analysis, use the Topic 1 skill instead.
  • mxup-fidelity-sparring
    Sparring partner for the fidelity block (Mobile UX Prototyping) – design question, chosen rung of the fidelity ladder, justification via the decision rule, and the self-named blind spot, as built in assignment 4 of exercise sheet U2. Use when a student wants these four elements tested for defensibility before Part B (upload Friday, 20 November), Part C or the seminar paper (self-assessment S4). For the slides and the fifteen minutes of Part B, use checkpoint-prep instead.
  • mxup-paper-check
    Interim skill for the seminar paper (Mobile UX Prototyping, about 5,000 words, due Friday 15 January 2027), into which all five exercise sheets U1–U5 go. Use when a student wants a draft passage checked criterion by criterion against the grading anchors S1–S12 of the self-assessment sheet, or wants to know which sheet's results feed which criterion.
