# How to use the interim skills

**What these are.** Three add-ons to your study partner (`mxup-study-partner`), one per pressure point of the semester. Each inherits the partner's rules – ask before answering, no finished solutions, every claim dated and sourced – and adds behavior for one specific situation. Using them is **voluntary**; they contain nothing that is not also in the course script, so nobody who works without them is at a disadvantage.

**The three, and when to switch them on:**

- **`expose-sparring/`** – from Topic 4 (when your design question exists) until the exposé deadline at the end of the Topic 5 week. It tests your four exposé elements (question, rung, justification, blind spot) for defensibility along claim → evidence → limit → price, asks exactly one question per round, and will not write a single exposé sentence for you. Retire it after submission.
- **`evidence-check/`** – from Exercise U1 onward, and for every market claim in your seminar paper until the end. It applies the three-part criterion (measurer, year, population), returns supported / supportable / unsupportable, flags undated states, and refuses to hand you sources – it checks the ones you hunted down yourself.
- **`defense-rehearsal/`** – from Topic 12 onward, in the weeks between the exercise on defending and your colloquium. It runs mock Q&A along the defense anatomy and the open question pool, asks the price question, and mirrors smooth answers back at you instead of letting them pass.

**Setting them up** (same three routes as the study partner):

- **Tools that read SKILL.md:** place the needed skill folder next to `mxup-study-partner/` where your tool loads skills. Keep the study partner installed – the interim skills inherit from it and are thinner alone.
- **Custom assistants with an instruction field:** paste the *body* of the relevant SKILL.md (everything below the second `---`) *after* the study partner's body in the instruction field; keep `referenz.md` as the knowledge file.
- **Plain chat, any provider:** attach the study partner's two files plus the one interim SKILL.md and write: "Act according to both SKILL.md files; referenz.md contains the module's definitions."

Run **one** interim skill at a time, matching the phase you are in – all three at once produces a partner that interrogates everything and helps with nothing.

**What to expect.** More refusal than usual, at the moments it hurts most: no exposé sentences before the exposé deadline, no sources during the source hunt, no model answers before the colloquium. That is not the skill failing – the exam tests the trace that building leaves in your thinking, and each refusal marks a spot where borrowed material could not be defended.

**Two honest notes**, unchanged from the study partner: no setup guarantees compliance – if the assistant slips into answer-machine mode, remind it of its role or restart. And whatever it says, the course script is the binding source; where they disagree, the script wins.

**Keep them current.** Version and date are in each SKILL.md header (v1.0, 2026-09-05). Updates appear in the learning platform alongside the study partner's.
