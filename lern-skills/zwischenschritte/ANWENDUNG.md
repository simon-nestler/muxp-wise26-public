# How to use the interim skills

**What these are.** Five add-ons to your study partner (`mxup-study-partner`), one per pressure point of the semester. Each inherits the partner's rules – ask before answering, no finished solutions, every claim dated and sourced – and adds behavior for one situation. Using them is **voluntary**; they contain nothing that is not also in the course script or the learning platform, so nobody who works without them is at a disadvantage.

**The five, and when to switch them on:**

- **`evidence-check/`** – from exercise sheet U1 onward, for every market claim you intend to use: the claim checks Part A presents, your slides for Parts A, B and C, your seminar paper. It applies the three-part criterion (measurer, year, population), returns supported / supportable / unsupportable, flags undated states, and refuses to hand you sources.
- **`checkpoint-prep/`** – in the days before the uploads for Part A (Friday, 23 October: the results of sheet U1) and Part B (Friday, 20 November: the results of sheets U2 and U3). It tests whether your slides show decisions rather than activity, asks one follow-up question per round, and asks what is still open.
- **`fidelity-sparring/`** – as soon as your design question exists (sheet U2, assignments 3 and 4). It tests the four elements of your fidelity block (question, rung, justification, blind spot) along claim → evidence → limit → price, one question per round, and will not write a sentence of them for you. The block goes into Part B, Part C and the seminar paper.
- **`defense-rehearsal/`** – before Part C, the final presentation (upload Friday, 18 December; 21 December or 11 January). It tests your core argument and talk across all five sheets, runs a one-on-one mock Q&A along the open question pool, and helps your team prepare and debrief the mock round from the learning platform. The mock round itself runs without AI. There is no rehearsal session in the timetable; you rehearse on your own.
- **`paper-check/`** – while you write the seminar paper (due Friday, 15 January 2027). It shows which sheet feeds which of the criteria S1–S12 of the self-assessment sheet, scores your pasted passage 2 / 1 / 0 as the sheet does, and names gaps without filling them.

**Topic skills** in `../themen/` go deeper on one topic: Topic 1 (the autopsy of the AI market analysis you generated yourself), Topic 9 (offline and sync, self-study this term) and Topic 10 (dark patterns).

**Setting them up** (same three routes as the study partner):

- **Tools that read SKILL.md:** place the skill folder next to the study partner's folder, named as in the `name` line of its header (for example `mxup-checkpoint-prep/`). Keep the study partner installed – the add-ons inherit from it and are thinner alone.
- **Custom assistants with an instruction field:** paste the *body* of the relevant SKILL.md (everything below the second `---`) *after* the study partner's body; keep `referenz.md` as the knowledge file.
- **Plain chat, any provider:** attach the study partner's two files plus the one SKILL.md and write: "Act according to both SKILL.md files; referenz.md contains the module's definitions."

Run **one** add-on at a time, matching the phase you are in – all of them at once produce a partner that interrogates everything and helps with nothing.

**What to expect.** More refusal than usual, at the moments it hurts most: no slides before Part A, B or C, no sentences for your fidelity block, no paper text, no sources during the source hunt, no quadrant sorting in the autopsy, no model answers to pool questions. That is not the skill failing – the assessment tests the trace that building leaves in your thinking, and each refusal marks a spot where borrowed material could not be defended.

**Two honest notes**, unchanged from the study partner: no setup guarantees compliance – if the assistant slips into answer-machine mode, remind it of its role or restart. And the course script, the exercise sheets and the learning platform are the binding sources; where they disagree with a skill, they win.

**Keep them current.** Version and date are in each SKILL.md header. Updates appear in the learning platform alongside the study partner's.
