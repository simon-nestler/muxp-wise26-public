# How to use the interim skills

**What these are.** Five add-ons to your study partner (`mxup-study-partner`), one per pressure point of the semester. Each inherits the partner's rules – ask before answering, no finished solutions, every claim dated and sourced – and adds behavior for one specific situation. Using them is **voluntary**; they contain nothing that is not also in the course script or the learning platform, so nobody who works without them is at a disadvantage.

**The five, and when to switch them on:**

- **`evidence-check/`** – from Exercise U1 onward, for every market claim on your sheets, your slides and in your seminar paper until the end. It applies the three-part criterion (measurer, year, population), returns supported / supportable / unsupportable, flags undated states, and refuses to hand you sources – it checks the ones you hunted down yourself.
- **`checkpoint-prep/`** – in the days before the uploads for Part A (Friday, 23 October) and Part B (Friday, 20 November). It tests whether your about ten slides show decisions rather than activity, asks one follow-up question per round, and asks what is still open. Paste the requirement text for the part from the learning platform; the skill will not guess it.
- **`expose-sparring/`** – from Topic 4 (when your design question exists) until the exposé deadline at the end of week 5. It tests your four exposé elements (question, rung, justification, blind spot) for defensibility along claim → evidence → limit → price, asks exactly one question per round, and will not write a single exposé sentence for you. Retire it after submission.
- **`paper-check/`** – from the interim state (end of the Topic 10 week) until you submit the seminar paper. It walks the twelve paper criteria S1–S12 of the self-assessment sheet one at a time, scores your pasted passage 2 / 1 / 0 as the sheet does, and names gaps without filling them.
- **`defense-rehearsal/`** – from the moment the question pool opens until your final presentation (Part C). There is no rehearsal session in the timetable; you rehearse on your own. The skill runs a one-on-one mock Q&A along the defense anatomy and the open pool, and helps your team prepare and debrief the mock round from the learning platform. The mock round itself runs without AI.

**Setting them up** (same three routes as the study partner):

- **Tools that read SKILL.md:** place the needed skill folder next to `mxup-study-partner/` where your tool loads skills. Keep the study partner installed – the interim skills inherit from it and are thinner alone.
- **Custom assistants with an instruction field:** paste the *body* of the relevant SKILL.md (everything below the second `---`) *after* the study partner's body in the instruction field; keep `referenz.md` as the knowledge file.
- **Plain chat, any provider:** attach the study partner's two files plus the one interim SKILL.md and write: "Act according to both SKILL.md files; referenz.md contains the module's definitions."

Run **one** interim skill at a time, matching the phase you are in – all five at once produces a partner that interrogates everything and helps with nothing.

**What to expect.** More refusal than usual, at the moments it hurts most: no slides before a checkpoint, no exposé sentences before the exposé deadline, no paper text before submission, no sources during the source hunt, no model answers before the final presentation. That is not the skill failing – the exam tests the trace that building leaves in your thinking, and each refusal marks a spot where borrowed material could not be defended.

**Two honest notes**, unchanged from the study partner: no setup guarantees compliance – if the assistant slips into answer-machine mode, remind it of its role or restart. And whatever it says, the course script and the learning platform are the binding sources; where they disagree with the skill, they win.

**Keep them current.** Version and date are in each SKILL.md header. Updates appear in the learning platform alongside the study partner's.
