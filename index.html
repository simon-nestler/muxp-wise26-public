  • mxup-topic9-offlineTopic skill for "Offline states and sync conflicts" (Topic 9, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use in addition to the module's study partner when working on offline triage, sync conflicts, or the offline part of Exercise U5.
  • mxup-topic10-darkpatternsTopic skill for "Dark patterns: naming and judging" (Topic 10, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use in addition to the module's study partner when working on dark patterns, the triple filing, the dark-pattern part of Exercise U5, or the Brussels-hearing preparation.
  • mxup-defense-rehearsalInterim skill for colloquium preparation (Mobile UX Prototyping). Use from Topic 12 onward when the student wants a mock Q&A on their seminar project before the 30-minute colloquium – questions along the defense anatomy and the open question pool.
  • mxup-evidence-checkInterim skill for evidence discipline (Mobile UX Prototyping). Use when the student wants their own claims checked against the module's three-part criterion – on exercise sheets from Exercise U1 onward, in the seminar paper, or anywhere a market claim appears.
  • mxup-expose-sparringMilestone skill for the exposé of the seminar paper (due end of week 5, Mobile UX Prototyping). Use when the student wants to test their design question and fidelity decision for defensibility before submitting the exposé.
